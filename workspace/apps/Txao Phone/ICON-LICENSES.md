# Icon licences

The platform logos in `app/src/main/res/drawable/ic_im_*.xml` (WhatsApp, Telegram, Signal, Messenger, Instagram, Discord, Snapchat, X, TikTok) are the path data of the matching [Simple Icons](https://simpleicons.org) glyphs (v13.21.0), converted to Android vector drawables and recoloured. Simple Icons is released under CC0 1.0 Universal (public domain dedication); the logos themselves remain trademarks of their owners and are shown only to identify each service.

The Skype and "Other" icons are original drawings, since Skype is no longer part of Simple Icons.
